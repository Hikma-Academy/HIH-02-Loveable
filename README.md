# 🚀 Getting Started with Lovable.dev

The free version of **Lovable.dev** is designed to help you test full-stack AI development capabilities, build prototypes, and validate application ideas without needing a credit card.

---

### 🔑 Key Features of the Free Tier

* **⚡ Daily Build Credits**
  You receive **5 build credits per day**. These reset daily at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over. On the free tier, these are capped at **30 credits per calendar month**.

* **🌐 Public Projects & Hosting**
  You can create and host up to **5 public projects** simultaneously on a `lovable.app` subdomain (e.g., `your-app.lovable.app`) for free.

* **🛠️ Full-Stack Prototyping**
  Get full access to the visual editor, pre-built templates, and auto-provisioned backend infrastructure via **Supabase** (enabling authentication, databases, and file storage).

* **🐙 GitHub Integration**
  Includes seamless version control integration, allowing you to track your version history and sync your code to your repositories.

* **👥 Unlimited Collaborators**
  Invite an unlimited number of team members to collaborate inside your free personal workspace.

> ⚠️ **Free Tier Limitations:** Includes read-only code access, no custom domains, public-only project visibility, and a "Built with Lovable" branding badge on your project.

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Guide: Create an Account & Get Started

#### 🟦 Step 1: Visit the Platform
Open your web browser and navigate to the official website at **[Lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev)**.

#### 🟦 Step 2: Sign Up
Click the **"Get started"** button in the top right corner of the homepage, then choose your registration method:
* **Social Authentication:** Sign up instantly using your **Google**, **GitHub**, or **Apple ID**.
* **Email & Password:** Enter your email address, create a password, and click **"Create your account"**. *(Make sure to click the verification link sent to your inbox to activate it!)*

#### 🟦 Step 3: Complete Onboarding
Once signed in, Lovable will guide you through a brief personalization menu:
1. Enter your name.
2. Choose your interface preference (**☀️ Light Mode** or **🌙 Dark Mode**).
3. Answer a couple of quick questions about your primary role (e.g., founder, hobbyist) and what you intend to build.

#### 🟦 Step 4: Build Your First App
1. From your new Lovable Dashboard, click **"New Project"** (or use the main prompt bar).
2. **Describe your idea** in plain English in the prompt box *(e.g., "Create a daily fitness habit tracker with a dark theme calendar heatmap")*.
3. Watch the AI write the code and assemble the visual components in real-time.
4. Use the **Chat panel on the left** to make text-based tweaks, or use the **Preview panel on the right** to interactively point-and-click elements you want to modify.
5. Click **"Publish"** in the top right to instantly generate a live URL and share your prototype!
